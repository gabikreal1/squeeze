# Squeeze — 3-Minute Demo Script

Mapped to Xero's published pitch structure: **0–30s hook · 30–100s demo · 100–150s tech · 150–180s future.** Demo business: *Maya's Catering Co.* (seeded Xero org).

---

## Slide 1 — Title (on screen during the hook)
**SQUEEZE** — *Squeeze your receivables before your payables squeeze you.* (capybara + juicer visual)

## 0:00–0:30 — The hook
> "A small business owner posted this on Reddit: *'I hate invoicing, so I avoid it.'* They're not lazy — chasing money from people you know is horrible. So invoices go out late, cash comes in late, and one day a big invoice gets paid *after* payroll. Big companies have a credit controller to prevent that. A 4‑person catering business has… the owner, at 9pm. **Meet Squeeze — an AI credit controller that lives on your Xero.**"

## 0:30–1:40 — The demo (live, this is what wins)
1. **Looks fine, isn't.** Maya's balance today: £2,100. "Looks healthy." → Squeeze: *"Actually, payroll's Friday — you'll be £1,180 under safe cash on Thursday."* (Show the Safe‑to‑Spend timeline dipping below the buffer line.)
2. **Why.** "Squeeze knows *why*: three unpaid invoices are due to arrive *after* the shortfall — and it predicts arrival from how each customer *actually* pays, which Xero's own forecast ignores."
3. **The judgment call (trust beat).** Open Patel's card: *"Patel always pays ~8 days late but always pays — don't squeeze, just nudge."* Then BrightBuild: *"First‑time customer, £2,400, 17 days overdue, ignored two emails — and it's the main cause of Thursday's gap."*
4. **Autonomous action.** Tap **Call**. Live AI call on speakerphone (teammate = accounts payable). AI discloses itself, asks for payment, gets a promise → logs it to Xero, sets the expected date.
5. **The heal.** Mark paid / fire the payment webhook → **Safe‑to‑Spend climbs back above the line, gap closed.** Say the number: *"£1,180 gap — gone. DSO down 6 days."*
6. **The department (quick flash).** Monday briefing: Treasury card (*"pay Henderson early = 18% annualised on idle cash"*) + Procurement card (*"waste supplier raised prices 14% — here's the email"*) + one revenue card (*"Riverside is high‑value and slowing — protect it"*).

## 1:40–2:30 — The tech
> "It's all Xero. We read invoices, contacts, payments, aged receivables and your bank balance; we write actions, notes and expected dates back. It's **webhook‑driven**, so a payment updates the forecast in real time. The forecast and the money math are deterministic code — the AI handles judgment, drafting and the call. And on trust: this is **B2B, your own invoices, AI disclosed, you approve every escalation, full audit trail in Xero** — it even prepares a protocol‑compliant Letter Before Action with statutory interest if it ever gets that far. It escalates *proportionately* — it knows when not to squeeze."

## 2:30–3:00 — The future
> "Squeeze is hire #1: the credit controller. Next, the same brain becomes your Treasury manager and your procurement analyst — a whole finance department. A 200‑person company pays £150k+ a year for that. We're giving it to 4.4 million small businesses for the price of a subscription. **Squeeze your receivables — before your payables squeeze you.**"

## Judging‑rubric cheat sheet
- **Human hours saved / impact** → "no more 9pm chasing; gap closed automatically; DSO −N days."
- **Creativity / wow** → live AI call + healing forecast + "knows when not to squeeze."
- **Usability** → owner just taps approve; UI is mobile + WhatsApp.
- **Technicality** → deterministic forecast + LLM agent + webhooks + write‑backs.
- **Business value / Xero central** → every step reads/writes Xero; targets the Revenue track head‑on.
- **Shippability** → scope‑honest, compliance handled, clear production path (Satago/FundTap, court filing).

## Backup plan on stage
- Live call fails → play recorded call.
- Network flakes → "demo mode" with seeded data + manual heal button.
- Keep a full screen‑recording ready to run end‑to‑end if all else fails.

## Numbers to have ready (measurable outcomes)
- £ gap identified → £ closed.
- DSO reduction (days).
- £ float captured re‑timing payables.
- £/yr recovered by procurement card.
